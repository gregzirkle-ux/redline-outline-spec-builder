# Architectural Specification Section Library

## Purpose and Scope

This is a comprehensive, architecture-focused section library organized using conventional CSI MasterFormat numbering. It is intended as a practical master checklist for commercial, hospitality, mixed-use, workplace, restaurant, retail, adaptive-reuse, and preservation projects.

It includes:

- Procurement and contracting requirements commonly assembled by the architect.
- Division 01 general requirements.
- Core architectural work in Divisions 02 through 14.
- Selected structural, fire-protection, plumbing, HVAC, electrical, communications, security, earthwork, exterior-improvement, and utility sections that commonly require architectural selection or coordination.

It is not the complete licensed CSI MasterFormat database or a substitute for MasterSpec, SpecLink, or office masters. Confirm final section numbers and titles against the project’s governing master, consultant scopes, owner requirements, and current CSI publications. Delete inapplicable sections and add project-specific sections as required.

- **Included** – Required in the Project Manual.
- **Possible** – Scope or selection remains unresolved.
- **Consultant** – Required but authored by another discipline.
- **Drawing Note Only** – Scope is intentionally documented without a full specification section.

For selected sections, track the responsible author, outline status, product basis, reference files, unresolved decisions, and final issue status.
